Unraveling the K+ transporter gene family in rice: evolution, subcellular localization, K+ uptake activity, and transcriptional responses to salt stress | 解析水稻 K+转运蛋白基因家族：进化、亚细胞定位、K+吸收活性以及对盐胁迫的转录响应

Abstract

Rice is a global staple food crop, and its yield is seriously limited by soil salinity. The high-affinity K + transporter/K + uptake permease/K + transporter (HAK/KUP/KT) family of potassium transporters plays a pivotal role in K + homeostasis and plant salt tolerance. However, their systematic characterization and functional relevance to salt tolerance in rice have not been clarified. Here, we conducted genome-wide identification and structural characterization of the HAK/KUP/KT family, assessed their expression profiles under salt stress, and examined their K + transport function. In total, 27 OsHAK genes were identified in the rice genome, most of which exhibited salt-responsive expression. Yeast functional complementation assays confirmed that OsHAK11, OsHAK13, OsHAK23, OsHAK24, OsHAK25, and OsHAK26 can mediate K + absorption. Among them, OsHAK11 and OsHAK25 showed relatively strong K + uptake ability. Subcellular localization analysis revealed that OsHAK11 and OsHAK25 were located in the plasma membrane. Our research systematically analyzed the sequence characteristics of the rice HAK gene family, examined their transcriptional responses to salt stress, and further evaluated the K + uptake capacity of HAK proteins. These results provide a foundation for further elucidating the function of OsHAKs in plant salt tolerance.

Blue light optimizes photosynthetic physiology and photoprotective molecular mechanisms of Hydrangea quercifolia seedlings | 蓝光优化栎叶绣球幼苗的光合生理与光保护分子机制

Abstract

Introduction Hydrangea quercifolia is a rare ornamental shrub whose natural populations have declined due to habitat loss, necessitating efficient artificial propagation techniques. Light quality is a critical environmental factor regulating seedling growth and physiological quality; however, the molecular mechanisms by which different LED light spectra modulate photosynthetic physiology and hormone signaling in H. quercifolia remain poorly understood. Methods We investigated the regulatory mechanisms of four LED light qualities—white (W), blue (B), red (R), and red:blue = 3:1 (R3B1)—on the growth, photosynthetic performance, chloroplast ultrastructure, hormone metabolism, and transcriptomic profiles of H. quercifolia 'Harmony' seedlings. Physiological parameters were measured on days 7, 14, and 21, and RNA sequencing was performed to identify differentially expressed genes and associated signaling pathways. Results Blue (B) light significantly increased chlorophyll, anthocyanin, and cytokinin (CTK) contents, promoted chloroplast proliferation and grana thylakoid development, and enhanced photosynthetic efficiency. Transcriptomic analysis revealed that B light upregulated genes encoding photosystem I (PSI) and photosystem II (PSII) reaction center proteins (especially PsbS, Photosystem II S subunit), strengthened non-photochemical quenching (NPQ) and photoprotective capacity, and reduced brassinosteroid (BR) biosynthesis by suppressing BRI1 (Brassinosteroid Insensitive 1) and BSK (Brassinosteroid-Signaling Kinase) expression. In contrast, R3B1 and red (R) light induced chloroplast shrinkage, reduced chloroplast number, and triggered starch accumulation. R3B1 light inhibited PSII oxygen evolution and electron transport, downregulating chloroplast-related genes; while R light suppressed cytokinin biosynthesis by downregulating CRE1 (Cytokinin Response 1) and activated BAK1 (BRI1-Associated Receptor Kinase 1)-mediated BR signaling. Discussion These findings demonstrate that blue light plays a pivotal role in promoting the high-quality development of hydrangea seedlings by optimizing photosynthetic apparatus assembly, enhancing photoprotection, and repressing BR-mediated growth inhibition. The differential molecular responses to distinct light spectra provide a theoretical basis for designing optimized artificial light environments and improving the efficiency of horticultural seedling production.

Coordination of lignin and pectin remodeling by a NAC transcription factor regulates seed abscission in sesame ( Sesamum indicum L .) | NAC 转录因子协调木质素和果胶重塑调控芝麻（Sesamum indicum L.）种子脱落

Abstract

Abstract Seed shattering, which facilitates seed dispersal, is essential for plant reproduction but causes substantial yield loss in sesame (Sesamum indicum L.). It occurs through capsule dehiscence and/or seed abscission from the placenta, either simultaneously or independently depending on the species. In this study, we investigated cell wall-associated factors underlying delayed seed abscission in the sesame cultivar Suwon195 (SW195) relative to the easy-shattering cultivar IT030100. Histological and transcriptome analyses indicated that cell wall remodeling, particularly involving pectin and lignin, contributes to the delayed abscission phenotype of SW195. SW195 showed higher pectin methylesterase activity and stronger accumulation of low-methyl-esterified homogalacturonan, together with lower polygalacturonase-associated pectin turnover, than IT030100. These changes are consistent with a pectin-remodeling state that favors wall stabilization in the SW195 abscission zone (AZ). AZ lignification was also weaker in SW195. Pharmacological inhibition of lignin biosynthesis altered pectin staining and reduced seed abscission in IT030100, whereas monolignol supplementation restored both phenotypes, supporting a functional association between lignification, pectin-related wall remodeling, and abscission. QTL mapping and whole-genome resequencing identified a major locus on chromosome 9 encompassing a NAC transcription factor (SiNAC; SIN_1025412) and a pectin methylesterase inhibitor (SiPMEI; SIN_1025380). Transient overexpression of SiNAC enhanced lignin biosynthesis in sesame. Together, these findings indicate that coordinated lignin deposition and pectin remodeling are associated with seed abscission in sesame and identify candidate genetic components for breeding reduced-shattering cultivars.

Genome sequencing and de novo assembly elucidate the genetic basis of grain quality and fragrance in heirloom rice cultivar Mushk Budji | 基因组测序与从头组装阐明传统水稻品种 Mushk Budji 中稻米品质和香味的遗传基础

Abstract

Background Mushk Budji is a premium, GI-tagged, heritage rice landrace of Kashmir valley, J&K, India, distinguished by its short-bold grains, strong aroma, and soft texture. While its morphological and biochemical traits have been documented, no dedicated genomic resource has previously been available. Here we report a de novo genome assembly of Mushk Budji with adequate depth. Methods We first-time performed reference guided whole-genome sequencing of Mushk Budji using the Illumina NovaSeq 6000 platform, generating 64.97 Gb of raw data. The genome assembly was achieved using SPAdes and scaffolded using RagTag against the Nipponbare reference. Structural and functional annotations were conducted with AUGUSTUS and curated across NCBI, UniProt, and KEGG databases. The haplotype analysis was performed for loci related to quality using a set of contrasting genotypes. Results and discussion The final assembly comprised 378.29 Mb with an N50 of 29.24 Mb and 97.0% BUSCO completeness. A total of 43,434 non-redundant protein-coding genes were identified, with significant enrichment in pathways related to aroma (2-acetyl-1-pyrroline) and starch biosynthesis. Allelic profiling confirmed the characteristic 8-bp deletion in Badh2, conferring fragrance, alongside additional genes shaping quality and adaptability. Unique haplotypes at SSI and RAmy1A, resembling those of temperate japonica variety Koshihikari, were associated with distinct soft and cohesive cooked texture of Mushk Budji . Comparative analyses revealed evolutionary divergence from canonical japonica references at specific loci. Selective signature analyses revealed contrasting evolutionary patterns among major quality genes. SSI showed evidence of balancing selection, whereas GS3 and Badh2 exhibited signatures of strong human-mediated selection, reflecting the historical preference for desirable grain quality and fragrance traits. Genes controlling amylose content and cooking quality (Wx and Alk) were under moderate purifying selection, indicating functional optimization. Conclusion The genomics resource generated in the present study establishes Mushk Budji as a distinct temperate japonica landrace with unique allelic combinations underpinning its aroma, texture and adaptability. The dataset provides a foundation for conservation, breeding and functional genomics, ensuring the sustainable utilization of this heritage rice.

Chloroplast Genome Evolution, Heteroplasmy, and Inverted Repeat Dynamics in the Elymus Complex (Triticeae, Poaceae): Insights from Single-Molecule Sequencing of Elymus ciliaris and Comparative Analysis of St-Genome Lineages | Elymus 复合体（Triticeae，Poaceae）中的叶绿体基因组进化、异质性与反向重复序列动态：基于 Elymus ciliaris 单分子测序及 St 基因组谱系比较分析的见解

Abstract

ABSTRACT Background/Objectives Elymus sensu lato (Poaceae) is arguably the largest and most complex genus in the tribe Triticeae. It includes hybrids and polyploids based on x=7 chromosomes, all including the St genome, forming a valuable genepool for forage grass and cereal breeding. Analysis of chloroplast genome diversity and structural dynamics is critical for resolving maternal lineages, reticulate evolution and biodiversity across this agronomically important complex, refining their taxonomy, conservation and exploitation. Methods We sequenced the complete chloroplast genome (plastome) of Elymus ciliaris (4x=2n=28; StStYY) using ultra-long Oxford Nanopore single-molecule reads and compared it to 77 additional chloroplast genomes representing major St-genome lineages in Elymus s.l. (Pseudoroegneria St; Elymus s.s. StH, StY; Thinopyrum StJ/E; Campeiostachys StYH; Kengyilia StYP). We analyzed structure, nucleotide diversity, inverted repeat (IR) dynamics, and phylogenetic signal. Results The E. ciliaris chloroplast genome was 135,008 bp long (38.3% GC) with a canonical quadripartite structure. Single-molecule reads (n=81) revealed heteroplasmy: two Small-Single-Copy (SSC) orientations at 30%:70% frequency, indicating an inversion polymorphism. Across the Elymus group, comparative analysis of chloroplast assemblies showed high structural conservation but lineage-specific IR-boundary shifts. Kengyilia exhibits exceptional IR expansion. Nucleotide diversity hotspots localize to the large single-copy region, especially in StY lineages. Phylogenies recover a monophyletic St-containing clade but do not delineate genera, reflecting reticulate evolution. Conclusions Single-molecule sequencing uncovered heteroplasmy with an inversion polymorphism in a single plant of Elymus ciliaris, hidden in short read assemblies. There were no other polymorphisms, as expected for chloroplast sequences. Our analyses showed that a Pseudoroegneria-like St chloroplast genome predominates as the maternal donor across Elymus polyploids. Variable regions and IR dynamics offer strong models for chloroplast genome evolution in reticulate lineages and suggest exploiting plastome variation to complement nuclear biodiversity studies.

Metabolic and signalling processes in plant cell wall integrity maintenance | 植物细胞壁完整性维持中的代谢与信号传导过程

Abstract

Abstract Background Our understanding of plant cell walls has profoundly changed in recent decades. Originally seen as sturdy recalcitrant structures supporting plant growth, development, and interactions with the environment, we now appreciate the dynamic processes and plasticity modulating cell wall homeostasis, enabling cell walls to perform their respective biological functions in a highly adaptive manner. A key component underlying plasticity and responsible for adaptive changes is the plant cell wall integrity maintenance mechanism. It is responsible for monitoring and maintaining the functional integrity of cell walls by initiating adaptive changes in cell wall composition and structure in response to stimuli arising during growth, development and interactions with the environment. Scope Here we provide an overview of our current knowledge of the processes important for plant cell wall structure and homeostasis in the context of cell wall integrity maintenance, summarizing the main components of the cell wall, the cell surface receptors responsible for monitoring the integrity of cell walls, and the mode of action of the cell wall integrity maintenance mechanism for preserving cell wall functions during physiological processes and stress responses. Conclusions This overview shows how the cell wall integrity maintenance mechanism ensures the functional integrity of the cell wall and how this is maintained through integration of different stimuli driving tightly controlled, adaptive changes in cell wall homeostasis by involving different receptors and signalling cascades.

摘要

背景 近几十年来，我们对植物细胞壁的理解已发生深刻变化。最初，细胞壁被视为支撑植物生长、发育以及与环境的相互作用的坚固且难以降解的结构；如今，我们认识到调节细胞壁稳态的动态过程和可塑性，使细胞壁能够以高度适应性的方式执行其各自的生物学功能。可塑性的一个关键组成部分，也是适应性变化的原因，是植物细胞壁完整性维持机制。该机制负责监测和维持细胞壁的功能完整性，通过响应生长、发育和与环境相互作用过程中产生的刺激，启动细胞壁组成和结构的适应性变化。范围 在此，我们概述当前关于植物细胞壁结构和稳态相关过程的知识，这些过程在细胞壁完整性维持的背景下尤为重要，总结细胞壁的主要组分、负责监测细胞壁完整性的细胞表面受体，以及细胞壁完整性维持机制在生理过程和胁迫响应期间保护细胞壁功能的作用方式。结论 本综述表明细胞壁完整性维持机制如何确保细胞壁的功能完整性，以及如何通过整合不同刺激来维持这一点，这些刺激通过涉及不同受体和信号级联，驱动对细胞壁稳态的严格控制的适应性变化。

Genotype-phenotype single-cell transcriptomics for massive parallel assessment of genetic variants | 用于大规模并行评估遗传变异的基因型-表型单细胞转录组学

Abstract

Predicting the pathogenic effect of rare genetic variants is significantly hampered by the limited cohort of diagnosed patients and the difficulty in functionally evaluating the effect of existing and novel variants. To address this issue, we leveraged a protein Multiplexed Assay of Variants Effect (MAVE) to functionally assess the effect of ~ 2200 missense variants of the TP63 gene, some of which cause autosomal dominant developmental disorders. The activity of each variant was measured using an optimized fibroblast-to-keratinocyte conversion protocol, and a subset of mutants was validated through multiple orthogonal biochemical and genomics functional assays to assess consistency and robustness. To systematically expand MAVEs beyond predefined assay, we here introduce SCRAMseq (Single Cell RNAseq Associated with MAVEs by sequencing). This generalizable platform leverages full-length scRNAseq for high-throughput, single-cell genotype-to-phenotype mapping. We validate SCRAMseq across different experimental and genetic contexts to demonstrate its reproducibility and accuracy. SCRAMseq provides a robust and versatile workflow poised to accelerate the discovery and dissection of gene variant effects on any potential disease-driving pathway, thereby significantly broadening MAVEs applicability to virtually any possible disease-driving gene. This work introduces SCRAMseq, a generalizable single-cell platform that pairs full-length transcriptomics with genotyping to test the functional effect of thousands of genetic variants simultaneously, bypassing the need for gene-specific functional assays.
